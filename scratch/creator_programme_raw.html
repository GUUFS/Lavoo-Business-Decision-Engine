Quick questions.

Do I need a big following?+

No. A small audience that trusts you often does better than a big one that scrolls past.

Does it cost anything to join?+

Nothing. You also get Premium free for your first 6 months.

Is there a target?+

No. Post when it suits you. The more people you bring in, the more you earn.

Do I earn on founding prices?+

Yes. You earn 50% of what each person actually pays. People who join before 31 January 2027 pay founding prices, so those payments are smaller. Everyone who joins after that pays regular prices.

What if someone pays for a whole year?+

You get 50% of the whole payment, once it clears. Quarterly payments work the same way.

When and how do I get paid?+

Every Friday, straight to your bank account, with no minimum. Each payment is paid once it clears.

Do I have to say I earn from it?+

Yes, every time. A simple "#ad" or "I earn if you join" is enough. It is the law, and it builds trust.

Where are the full terms?+

In our short, plain-English Creator Agreement. You sign it once you are accepted.
